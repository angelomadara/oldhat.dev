Hello, I am Oleg Full Stack Developer.

Honestly, I’m still figuring out what exactly I want to put on this website. I just built it because, well, why not? Sometimes I dive into projects without a clear plan, just following the noise inside my brain that never seems to quiet down. Maybe one day I’ll turn this space into something meaningful or showcase some cool work, but for now, it’s just here—raw, simple, and a little bit chaotic, just like me. If you’re looking for someone to blame, I’d say you can safely point your finger at my brain that always seems to have a thousand tabs open at once.

You might be wondering why I built this website using plain and simple HTML instead of using a modern, complex framework like React, Vue, or Next.js. The answer is simple—sometimes simplicity is exactly what I need. I wanted this site to be as lightweight, minimal, and distraction-free as possible. No fancy builds, no dependencies, no extra layers—just pure, straightforward HTML and CSS that loads fast and gets the message across.
