Reading these bars

Human traffic usually follows the local day — quiet overnight, busier in the evening. Bot traffic does not sleep: a flat band of requests across the small hours is the normal background radiation of the public internet. Spikes in the bot bars at a single hour often line up with scans visible in the path activity for the same date.
