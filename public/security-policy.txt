# Security Policy — oldhat.dev

## Reporting

Please send security reports to admin@oldhat.dev.
For encrypted communication: https://keybase.io/angelomadara/pgp_keys.asc

## Scope

- The static site oldhat.dev and its Nginx configuration
- The FastAPI service at api.oldhat.dev
- The open-source frontend repository github.com/angelomadara/oldhat.dev

## What we do with reports

- Every report is read and acknowledged, usually within a few days.
- Reports are kept confidential until the issue is fixed and verified.
- Nothing about a report is published without the reporter's permission.
- This is a personal, hobby-run project: there is no bug bounty programme
  and no financial reward is offered.

## Guidance

Please include reproducible steps and the affected URL or endpoint.
Note that the traffic data published on this site is intentionally public;
privacy reports about that data are still welcome.
