What the numbers mean

A healthy static site is dominated by 2xx and 304 responses. A large 4xx share is expected here: it is the sound of automated scanners asking for files that were never there — /.env, /wp-login.php, /.git/config. Any sustained 5xx traffic is the one thing worth investigating, since it means the server itself failed to answer.
